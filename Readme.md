We are a dedicated food-tech platform designed specifically for the tertiary education landscape in Botswana. By digitizing the traditional meal plan, we bridge the gap between local campus kitchens and students at institutions like UB and BAC.Our platform offers a recurring 30-day "Lunch Only" subscription, allowing students to secure their daily meals through integrated local payment solutions like Orange Money, FNB, and Pula transfers. We aren't just a website; we are a logistics partner committed to reducing queues, eliminating payment friction, and ensuring every student has access to a nutritious meal without the midday stress.
