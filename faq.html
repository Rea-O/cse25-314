Frequently Asked Questions

How does the subscription work?

Choose between our 5-day (P500) or 7-day (P600) monthly plans. You'll receive one delicious meal per day according to our weekly menu. Payment is made monthly in advance.

What time is delivery?

Meals are delivered between 12:00 PM - 2:00 PM to UB, BAC, and IDM campuses. We'll coordinate specific pickup points with you after subscription.

Can I order just one meal without subscribing?

Yes! We offer daily plates at P30 each. Contact us via the Contact page or WhatsApp to place your order.

How do I pay?

We accept cash, bank transfer (FNBB/ABSA), and mobile money (Orange Money). Payment details are provided once you submit a request.
